# Born-Agentic Organization Manifesto

## 1. Stop treating AI as an add-on

Most enterprise AI initiatives begin with an organization that already exists. People, departments, processes, software and habits already exist. AI arrives last.

Born-Agentic Organization starts from a different assumption:

> A company designed for the Agent era should not need to be "AI transformed" later.

AI should be part of the organizational structure from day zero.

## 2. Roles should exist before employees

A company does not fundamentally need a person first. It needs a capability, responsibility and outcome.

Those can be represented as a persistent Role.

The Role should exist independently of the current employee, model, tool and workflow engine.

Humans and agents may enter and leave. The Role persists.

## 3. Agents should be able to work before humans are hired

If a Role Agent can perform research, execution, coordination and follow-up, it should begin working immediately.

Human labor should not be the default starting condition of every Role.

A human should be added when the Role detects that human capability is needed.

## 4. Capability before headcount

Traditional planning asks: **How many people do we need?**

Born-Agentic planning asks: **What capabilities are required to achieve the objective?**

Then:
- What can the resident Agent do?
- What can software do?
- What can an API do?
- What can another Agent do?
- What can be outsourced?
- What requires a Human?

Headcount becomes an output of capability allocation, not its starting point.

## 5. A Role can ask for a Human

The most important inversion is this:

> Hiring can be initiated by the Role.

A persistent Role Agent observes its own performance and identifies a capability gap. It can ask a People Agent to find a person with the required abilities.

## 6. Humans join running Roles

A new employee should not receive an empty workstation and a folder of onboarding documents.

They should enter a Role that already knows what has happened, what is happening now, what worked, what failed and what the new human is expected to contribute.

## 7. Organizations should not forget when people leave

Employees are temporary participants. Organizational memory should not be.

When a human leaves, the Role retains state, history, decisions, artifacts, lessons, relationships and pending work.

## 8. Humans remain accountable

Born-Agentic does not mean "human-free."

It means humans are no longer assumed to be the default execution layer for every organizational function.

Human accountability remains explicit for high-impact decisions, including employment, legal commitments, major financial actions and safety-critical outcomes.

## 9. The company becomes a runtime

A Born-Agentic company is not a collection of chatbots.

It is an event-driven organizational runtime in which persistent Roles observe, plan, execute, remember, evaluate, escalate, request capability and coordinate with Humans and Agents.

## Thesis

> **Roles exist before employees. Agents start working before humans are hired. Humans join when the organization needs capabilities that machines cannot reliably or economically provide.**

# Governance Principles

Born-Agentic systems should not equate autonomy with absence of accountability.

Minimum governance principles:

- High-impact employment decisions require Human accountability.
- Legal commitments require explicit authority.
- Major financial actions require policy-controlled approval.
- Every autonomous action should be attributable to a Role and Agent runtime.
- Role authority must be separable from model capability.
- Human employee monitoring must have clear privacy boundaries.
- Organizational memory must support retention and deletion policy.
- Model replacement must not silently change Role authority.
- Critical decisions require audit trails and rollback where possible.

These principles are the counterpart to core principle 7, **Human Accountability**: an Agent may plan, execute and recommend, but high-impact employment, legal, financial and safety decisions keep an explicit human owner.
